# What's New in TypeThru

TypeThru shows the versions you skipped after an update. Newest first; each `##` heading is one
version, and the app reads this file, so keep the format.

## 0.6.1 · 1 November 2026

- **Settings closes again**: the close button of the Settings window works, and on a small screen
  the window fits and its options scroll.
- **What's New**: after an update, TypeThru shows what changed in every version since yours.
  Open it again from **Settings › Help › What's New**.
- **Read before you update**: the update notice has a **What's New** button with the notes of
  every version you would get.

## 0.6 · 1 November 2026

- **Copy Text from Screen** (Control+]): drag over any text you can see, even in a remote
  session or a picture, and it lands on your clipboard. It is read on your Mac and the picture
  is deleted at once. macOS asks once for Screen Recording permission.
- **Bold and italic**: Markdown such as `**bold**` and `*italic*` from chat and AI tools is typed
  as real bold and italic. Choose **Type as is** or **Remove the marks** in **Settings › Typing**.
  Run **Set Up** once more so the helper can press Control+B and Control+I.
- **Light and dark mode**: the panel, Settings, and alerts follow your Mac's appearance.
- **New install window** with just TypeThru and Applications.
- **No surprise commands**: a line break at the end of the copied text is not typed, so a copied
  command waits for you to press Return.

## 0.5.1 · 15 October 2026

- **Light and dark mode** for the panel, Settings, and alerts.
- **New install window** with just TypeThru and Applications.
- **No surprise commands**: a line break at the end of the copied text is not typed.

## 0.5 · 14 October 2026

- **Install updates in the app**: choose **Install** in the panel. TypeThru checks the update's
  signature, replaces itself, reopens, and keeps the Accessibility permission.
- **Open at login**: TypeThru asks once; change it any time in Settings.
- **Glass Settings** to match the panel.
- **Line breaks stay in place** in remote apps that handled a new line late.

## 0.4 · 7 October 2026

- **Settings window** with open at login, your shortcut, typing method, speed, and diagnostics.
- **Update notices**: TypeThru checks GitHub at most once a day and shows when a new version is out.
- **Your own shortcut**, with warnings about keys that also reach the remote session.
- **Safer setup**: setup files are checked against fingerprints before anything runs as
  administrator, and the driver must be signed by its developer.

## 0.3 · 6 October 2026

- **New name and look**: KeyTyper is now TypeThru, with a Liquid Glass panel.
- **Setup inside the app** with the standard macOS password prompt.
- **Five typing speeds**, from an average typist to 600 words per minute.
- **Symbols without a key**, such as bullets and smart quotes, are typed as plain keys.
- **Line breaks as Shift+Return**, so chat apps make a new line instead of sending.
- **Stops instantly** on Esc, a mouse click, or switching apps.

## 0.1 · 6 October 2026

- **First beta**, as KeyTyper: types the clipboard as key presses with Control+\.
